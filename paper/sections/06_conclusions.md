## 6. Conclusions

On a 1 m grid of the Gale boxwork, an exact walkback planner with directed metabolic costs never calls an unsafe goal safe, while fixed-radius and constant-rate rules call 21–46 % of their goals safe when they are not. Inside a 2 × 2 km box, time binds before energy, and service at the stations dominates the cost of a tour. Curiosity's traverse shows that the orbital DEM explains where to go but not how to move inside the boxwork; only sand mapped from the orthophoto earns a price, and a small one. Radio line of sight, light and an adaptive reserve are cheap to compute on the same grid and change what an astronaut should be told during the walk. The planner runs offline and on site, with the guarantees and limits stated above.
