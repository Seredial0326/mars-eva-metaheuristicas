      About this view
      Gale boxwork, 2 × 2 km.
      Mouse: drag to rotate around the point under the cursor, right-drag (or Shift + drag) to move, wheel to zoom.
      Touch: one finger rotates, two fingers move, pinch to zoom, twist to turn.
      Compass: face north. Frame (four corners): reset the view.
      Rehearsing the route: Play gets keyboard focus when you enter. Tab moves through the main buttons, More, Panel, Exit, then the other visible controls; Shift + Tab goes back. Space plays or pauses when focus is outside a control; on a button or field it keeps that control's usual action. More and Panel keep focus on their button when opened or closed. Esc closes the top card or message and pauses. Exit leaves and returns focus to Rehearse.
      Drag to look around, double-click to look forward again. Click a pin for its card.
      Walking freely: W/S or Up/Down to walk forward/back, A/D to step sideways, Left/Right arrows to turn (also while walking), click the view and move the mouse to look, Shift to run, F for fast travel, C for the view from behind, Esc to leave.
      The boxwork network is seen in the HiRISE photo (0.25 m). The relief (1 m DEM) shows the hillside, knolls and scarps; it does not resolve the 1–2 m ridges. The photo has its own light (HiRISE takes its images in mid-afternoon); the 3D Sun is added on top. Shadows are drawn for the relief as shown (true only at ×1) and they show knolls and scarps, not the 1–2 m boxwork ridges.
